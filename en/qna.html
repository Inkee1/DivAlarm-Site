Technical
Why are RSI values slightly different from TradingView or other platforms?

RSI values use an average of 14 candles, and the starting point of the calculation can cause slight differences in values.

Additionally, there are two main calculation methods: EMA (Exponential Moving Average) and SMA (Simple Moving Average).

DivAlarm calculates RSI in a way that better reflects recent trends, which may result in minor variations compared to other platforms.

Account
Can only one person use the app per account?

Yes, signals are primarily sent to the most recently logged-in device.

Some signals may still be received on multiple devices if logged in simultaneously, but for reliable operation, we recommend using the device where you last logged in.

If you experience issues, go to Settings → Sync Settings in the app and tap "Receive on this device" to ensure proper functionality.

Apple's iOS has much stricter policies compared to Android.

Features
What does "1m/1m" mean in DivForming?

The 1m/1m setting triggers an alert the moment a divergence is about to form on the 1-minute chart.

This means you'll receive an alert whenever the candle trend and RSI trend start to diverge — catching potential reversals at the earliest possible moment.

This is ideal for traders who want to be notified before the divergence is fully confirmed.

Technical
What is RSI Divergence and why is it important?

RSI Divergence occurs when the price movement and RSI indicator move in opposite directions.

Bullish Divergence: Price makes a lower low, but RSI makes a higher low → potential upward reversal

Bearish Divergence: Price makes a higher high, but RSI makes a lower high → potential downward reversal

RSI divergence is widely recognized as one of the most reliable indicators for predicting market reversals in technical analysis.

Features
How does TradingView Webhook integration work?

With Pro subscription, you receive a unique webhook URL that connects DivAlarm to TradingView.

Features include:

• Powerful phone alarms that bypass silent mode
• Composite signals — combine multiple alerts into one
• Auto-order execution on supported exchanges

Simply paste your webhook URL in TradingView's alert settings, and include specific keywords in your alert message to trigger the corresponding action.

Features
How does Auto-Order work?

Auto-Order (Pro only) automatically executes trades when your selected TradingView alerts fire.

Supported on up to 5 exchanges, it allows you to:

• Set up buy/sell orders triggered by specific alert keywords
• Configure order size, type, and conditions
• Execute trades even when your phone is in your pocket

Note: Auto-order requires proper API key setup with your exchange.
