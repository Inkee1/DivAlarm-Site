Send TradingView alerts to your phone

Paste one address into TradingView, then send a test from this page. You will see exactly where the signal stops if something is wrong.

  1. Copy your webhook URL

    This address is yours alone. Every TradingView alert that uses it goes to the DivAlarm app on your phone.

    Sign in to see your URL

    Keep it private. Anyone with this URL can send alerts to your phone.

  2. Paste it into a TradingView alert

    On a TradingView chart, right-click and choose Add alert. Open Notifications, check Webhook URL, paste your URL and press Apply.

    New alerts usually remember the last URL, so you only paste it once.

  3. Write a message with your keyword

    DivAlarm reacts to the keywords you set in the app under Keyword alarm → Webhook keywords. Put one of them in the alert Message. For example, with the keyword AA:

    AA {{ticker}} {{close}}

    TradingView replaces {{ticker}} and {{close}} with the symbol and price when the alert fires.

  4. Create the alert

    Press Create. When the alert fires, your phone rings with the alarm you chose for that keyword, and any auto-order linked to it runs.

    Before trusting it with orders, send a test from this page to confirm your phone is reachable.
